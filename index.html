About

The engineer behind the services.

I started on the integration side — wiring REST and SOAP APIs together for an insurance claims platform — before moving into architecting the services themselves. That order matters: I've felt what a badly designed contract costs downstream, so I design schemas and service boundaries to avoid it in the first place.

Most of my work sits in system design, event-driven architecture, and API optimization — the parts of a product users never see but always feel when they're slow. Lately that's meant Kafka-based pipelines, Redis caching, and pulling response times down without touching a UI at all.

I still keep a foot in the frontend, building the React.js interfaces that sit on top of the services I design, so I can reason about a feature end-to-end instead of handing it off halfway through.
